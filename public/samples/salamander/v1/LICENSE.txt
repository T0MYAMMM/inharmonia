Subset taken from the Tone.js audio repository (https://github.com/Tonejs/audio),
one sample every minor third from A0 to C8.

Modifications for Inharmonia: leading silence trimmed, truncated to 8 seconds
with a 1.5 second fade-out, re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis (q1) and MP3 (64 kbps).
